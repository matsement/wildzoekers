Why is this bumblebee flying here?

He was attracted to the flowers in the low vegetation...

▼

🍯 Why is he foraging here?

Bumblebees eat nectar and pollen from flowers. They need a lot of energy to keep their body temperature warm.

▼

🌍 What does he do for nature?

Bumblebees help plants grow. While foraging in flowers, they carry pollen to other flowers. This helps plants and fruit grow better.

▼

⚠️ Why not pick flowers?

Many people pick flowers. Nice for at home, but without flowers, bumblebees cannot feed and cannot build nests...
