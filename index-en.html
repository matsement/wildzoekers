Wildzoekers: The interactive monitoring platform for urban nature.

Big changes start small. Wildzoekers is an advanced citizen-science monitoring platform, seamlessly wrapped inside an immersive exploration game. We activate, connect, and educate local communities to collaboratively track and protect the hidden biodiversity thriving within intentionally wild urban spaces.

Why does this matter?

Wild nature in urban environments is frequently overlooked, dismissed as "untamed weeds", or misidentified as simple maintenance neglect. But appearances can be deceptive.

These untouched, rugged verges and spontaneous plants serve as the literal powerhouse of our city's biodiversity. Insects, birds, and small mammals rely on these areas for essential food sources and nesting habitats. To preserve them effectively, we must first map what lives there.

How does Wildzoekers work?

Our platform bridges the gap between the physical outdoor environment and digital citizen science through three simple, interactive steps:

1

Spot the Signboard & 3D Insects

Around the neighborhood, a physical signboard instantly catches your eye. Tucked away close by, unique and tangible 3D-printed insects are hidden, inviting passersby to take action.

2

Search, Scan, and Collect Playfully

The quest begins! Residents go on a micro-adventure to locate the hidden creatures. By scanning their custom codes via smartphone, users capture and unlock the insects into their digital collection.

3

Monitor, Report, and Learn Together

This is where real impact is made. Through the digital platform, users upload live photo observations and log local insect counts, building a meaningful research database while learning about ecosystems.

Why Wildzoekers works

Education and Communication

The platform transforms invisible ecological data into tangible community knowledge. Through transparent communication and targeted insights, neighbors discover that these "untamed" patches are actually vital, intentionally engineered biodiversity hotspots.

Community Participation

Wildzoekers converts passive passersby into active citizen scientists. By crowdsourcing environmental observations, exchanging photos, and tracking neighborhood wildlife, residents cultivate a shared sense of neighborhood pride and stewardship.

Playful Discovery

By framing rigorous environmental monitoring within a challenging, beautifully stylized game, we lower the entry barrier significantly. The process of searching, scanning, and accumulating data keeping the platform dynamic, engaging, and highly accessible for all demographics.

About this project

Wildzoekers was developed as part of a TU/e Industrial Design Final Bachelor Project by Mats van Deijck.

This academic design exploration investigates how interactive architecture and citizen-science infrastructures can fundamentally improve public perception and long-term acceptance of intentional, wild urban landscaping.

This platform is a functional prototype currently undergoing active field testing.
