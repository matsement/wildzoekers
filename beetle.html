Ecological role on campus

🍽️ Why does this beetle graze here?

🌍 What is its main contribution to the ecosystem?

⚠️ Why does TU/e choose to leave dead wood in place?

  • 🍽️ It eats the fungi inside the wood

    This beetle doesn't feed on the wood itself — it eats the fungi growing inside it. Those fungi slowly break down the dead wood and make it nutrient-rich for the beetle.

  • 🔄 It returns nutrients to the soil

    By breaking down dead wood alongside fungi, the beetle releases important nutrients back into the soil. This keeps the campus ecosystem in balance and supports other plants and animals.

  • 🪦 Dead wood is a crucial habitat

    A fallen tree may look like waste, but it's actually a new beginning. TU/e deliberately leaves it in place because it provides a home for thousands of organisms — from insects to fungi to birds in search of food.
