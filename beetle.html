Why is this beetle here?

It was drawn to the dead wood...

▼

🍽️ Why does it graze here?

Beetles don't just eat wood — they eat the fungi that grow inside dead wood. These fungi break down the wood and make it nutrient-rich.

▼

🌍 What does it do for nature?

Beetles help spread fungi, which breaks down dead wood and returns nutrients to the soil. They're also food for many animals like birds, helping keep nature healthy and in balance.

▼

⚠️ Why leave dead wood alone?

Many people want to clean up dead wood because it looks 'messy'. But removing it destroys a home for thousands of organisms, including beetles. The municipality makes this choice deliberately.
