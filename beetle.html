Why is this beetle here?

It was drawn to the dead wood...

TU/e: "Dead wood is not litter. It is a valuable habitat that we deliberately leave behind to promote biodiversity on our green campus."
▼

🍽️ Why does it graze here?

Beetles don't just eat wood — they eat the fungi that grow inside dead wood. These fungi break down the wood and make it rich in nutrients.

▼

🌍 What does it do for nature?

Beetles help spread fungi, which breaks down dead wood and returns nutrients to the soil. They're also an essential food source for birds and other animals, keeping the ecosystem in balance.

▼

⚠️ Why not remove the dead wood?

Dead wood can look messy, which makes people want to clear it away. But removing it destroys a home for thousands of organisms, including beetles. TU/e deliberately chooses to leave it — precisely because of how ecologically valuable it is.
