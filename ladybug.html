Why is this ladybug here?

She was attracted to flowers and foliage...

▼

🐛 Why is she grazing here?

Ladybugs eat dozens of aphids a day. This ladybug is hunting for these aphids on the flowers and leaves.

▼

🌍 What does she do for nature?

Ladybugs are a natural pest controller. By eating aphids, they help keep plants healthy. Without ladybugs, aphids could destroy vegetation.

▼

⚠️ Why not remove 'weeds'?

Many people remove these 'weeds'. For ladybugs and other insects, this is catastrophic! Without food and shelter, they cannot survive.
